Cartoon Creator & Production Library

A practical guide to the documented cartoon-making workflow and the built-in production assets available to support scenes, sound, and finishing.

Scope note: This guide describes features and starter assets present in the project source. It is not a live inventory of every generated, uploaded, or project-specific asset. The built-in production pack contains general production materials; not every item is cartoon-specific.

Cartoon Creator at a glance

Start with a story

Begin with an idea in Kannada or English, then build the screenplay, storyboard, and shots in Film Maker.

Guided workflow

The Cartoon Creator documents a 17-step guided workflow for creating, reviewing, and exporting a film.

Scene editing

Generate a film preview, add scenes to the editing timeline, review the result, and complete quality checks before export.

Final approval

The final preview must be watched and explicitly approved before rendering. The export step provides a verified MP4 for download.

Documented final workflow steps

The repository documentation names the final six steps as follows; it does not enumerate the individual labels for steps 1–11.

Step 12 · Generate previewCreate the film preview in Cartoon Creator.
Step 13 · Build the timelineAdd the scenes to the editing timeline.
Step 14 · Quality checksComplete the production quality checks.
Step 15 · Watch & approveWatch the final preview and explicitly approve it.
Step 16 · RenderRender the approved film.
Step 17 · DownloadDownload the verified MP4.
